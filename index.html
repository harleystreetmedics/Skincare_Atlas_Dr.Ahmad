What is included in the Atlas?

9 original reference sheets, organised into three chapters, and three companion worksheets.

Can I print the sheets or save a PDF?

Yes. Download the full Atlas as a PDF, or open an individual guide and select “Print / save PDF”.

Does this provide a diagnosis or treatment plan?

No. The sheets provide general educational context and consultation questions. They do not establish suitability, prescribe care or replace an individual clinical assessment.

Where are my saved sheets stored?

Only in this browser on this device. No account is required. Clearing browser data removes saved selections. No medical history is collected by this library.
